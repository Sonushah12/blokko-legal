# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blokko ("the app", "we", "us"), published by TwiSon Studio, handles information when you play. Blokko has no accounts and no servers of its own.

## Information stored on your device
- Your best score, sound/haptics settings and whether you bought "Remove Ads" are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Advertising
- Blokko shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads, to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.
- If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
- On iOS, the app asks permission before your device's advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Leaderboards
- If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), Blokko sends your best score to that service's leaderboard. Your gamer name, avatar and score are then visible to other players. Sign-in and the leaderboard are run by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; we never receive your account details.
- You can sign out, or change who sees your profile, in the Google Play Games app or in iOS Settings → Game Center.

## Purchases
- "Remove Ads" is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Their privacy policies apply to the purchase.

## Children
- Blokko is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your rights
- Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we hold no data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google using the links above. You can also contact us and we will help.

## Changes
- We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
